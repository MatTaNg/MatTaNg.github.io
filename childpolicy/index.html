Child Safety Standards

Effective date: January 1, 2026 · Legal entity: Amoraea

Amoraea ("Amoraea," "we," "us," or "our") operates a social and dating mobile application. We are committed to keeping our platform free of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and to protecting the safety of children. This page describes our published standards and practices in accordance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

1. Zero tolerance for CSAE

Amoraea has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We explicitly prohibit users from creating, uploading, sharing, soliciting, or distributing any content or behavior that facilitates the exploitation or abuse of children, including but not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Child grooming, including befriending a minor to facilitate sexual contact or exchange of sexual imagery
  • Sexualization of a minor, including imagery that depicts, encourages, or promotes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children
  • Inappropriate sexual interaction targeted at a child
  • Sextortion involving minors
  • Trafficking or solicitation of a child for commercial sexual exploitation
  • Any other conduct that endangers or exploits children

Users who violate these standards will be removed from the App and may be reported to law enforcement and relevant authorities.

2. Age requirement

The Amoraea App is intended only for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly permit users under 18 to create accounts or use the App. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will terminate the account and delete associated data promptly. See our Privacy Policy for additional details.

3. In-app reporting and user feedback

Amoraea provides an in-app mechanism for users to submit feedback, concerns, and safety reports. Users can report profiles, messages, or other content that they believe violates our standards or endangers others directly within the App. Reports are reviewed by our team, and we take appropriate enforcement action in accordance with these standards.

Users may also contact us at any time using the email address in Section 6 below.

4. Response to child sexual abuse material (CSAM)

Upon obtaining actual knowledge of CSAM or CSAE-related content on our platform, Amoraea will take prompt and appropriate action, including:

  • Removing the reported content without delay
  • Disabling or permanently banning the responsible account(s)
  • Preserving relevant evidence as required by law
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline, or to the appropriate regional authority where applicable
  • Cooperating with law enforcement as required

We do not allow CSAM on our platform under any circumstances.

5. Compliance with child safety laws

Amoraea complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where we operate. This includes maintaining procedures to report confirmed CSAM to NCMEC or other relevant regional authorities, responding to valid legal requests, and cooperating with investigations related to child safety.

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement first. You may also report concerns to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org (U.S.) or an equivalent authority in your country.

6. Child safety point of contact

Amoraea has designated a child safety point of contact to receive notifications from Google Play and other parties regarding potential CSAE content found in our App or on our platform. This representative is authorized to speak to our enforcement and review procedures and to take action when required.

Child safety contact: admin@amoraea.com
Legal entity: Amoraea

7. Enforcement and account actions

Violations of these standards may result in warnings, content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent account termination at our sole discretion. We may also report violations to law enforcement and child protection organizations. We review reports as quickly as practicable and prioritize matters involving child safety.

8. Updates to these standards

We may update this page from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, applicable law, or platform requirements. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

9. Contact us

For general questions, visit our Contact page. For child safety matters, email admin@amoraea.com.
