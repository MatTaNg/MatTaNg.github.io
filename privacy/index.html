Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Amoraea ("Amoraea," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, date of birth, and any other profile information you choose to provide.

Interview content. The App includes a guided conversational interview ("Aria") that asks about your relationship experiences and preferences. In the course of this interview we collect:

  • Audio recordings of your spoken responses, where you use voice input
  • Text transcripts of your responses
  • Scores and assessments derived from your responses relating to relationship-relevant traits (for example, communication style, conflict repair, and emotional attunement)

This is sensitive information. We collect it only to power the matching features of the App, and we describe how it's used and protected below.

Usage and device information. We automatically collect information about how you use the App, including device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, and general usage analytics.

Information from other users. If you interact with other users through the App (for example, messaging a match), we collect the content of those interactions.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate the App and conduct your Aria interview
  • To generate compatibility assessments and suggest matches
  • To maintain and improve the accuracy and safety of our matching system
  • To communicate with you about your account or the App
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or safety issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your interview content or derived scores for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How we share your information

Service providers. We work with third-party vendors who process data on our behalf, including cloud hosting providers, AI and language-model providers used to conduct and score your interview, and speech-to-text or text-to-speech providers used to power voice conversations. These providers are contractually restricted from using your data for any purpose other than providing services to us.

Other users. Limited profile information is shared with other users as part of the App's matching functionality. Your raw interview transcripts and audio recordings are not shared with other users.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of any person, investigate fraud, or address security issues.

Business transfers. If Amoraea is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Data retention

We retain your account information and interview data for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period after deletion as necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your data at any time — see Section 6.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, including your interview recordings, transcripts, and derived scores
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address on our Contact page. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to, and may not be used by, anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from a user under 18, we will delete it.

8. International users

If you access the App from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your home jurisdiction.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by other means before the changes take effect. The "Effective date" above reflects the latest revision.

10. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices can be directed to us via our Contact page.
