Request account & data deletion

Amoraea lets you request permanent deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it. You do not need the mobile app installed to submit a request.

How to request deletion

Email admin@amoraea.com from the phone number or email on your Amoraea account. Include:

  • The subject line Amoraea account deletion request
  • The phone number and/or email used to sign up
  • Any other detail that helps us verify it is your account
Email deletion request

What we delete

After we verify your request, we permanently delete your Amoraea account and associated app data, including profile information, interview history, assessment results, photos, and related records we store for matching.

Timing & retention

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Limited data may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution, as described in our Privacy Policy.
